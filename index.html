03 · Awareness

Cybersecurity Corner

A few practical tips I follow and share with others.

01

Strong, unique passwords

Use long passphrases (12+ characters) mixing words, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse a password across accounts.

02

Turn on 2FA everywhere

Two-factor authentication is the single easiest way to stop most account takeovers — enable it on email, banking, and social apps.

03

Spot phishing attempts

Be cautious of urgent messages asking you to click links or share details. Always check the sender before trusting a message.

04

Hack ethically, always

Only test systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Unauthorized access is illegal, regardless of intent.

05

Keep everything updated

Most real-world breaches exploit old, unpatched software. Regular updates close known security gaps.
